# 写法卡（立母版的提示词；照 GPT Image 官方提示词写法）

> 官方写法要点：按「场景 → 主体 → 关键细节 → 不许改的」顺序写，连贯的句子；多张参考图用「Image 1、Image 2」点名说清各是什么；要保持一致就明说 preserve／do not change，并列出不许改的项；改图时每一轮都把不许改的项重说一遍（防止越改越走样）；要字就把字放进引号、写清字体和位置。
> 下面模板里 `{…}` 换成这单的内容；`{不许改}` 一律从身份证「不许改」一节**原文**贴进来，一字不改。英文写给引擎，中文说明给丹青看。

## 一、商品四面板（参考图：Image 1＝主人的商品原图，有第二张就 Image 2）
```
A clean product reference sheet on a pure white background, arranged as a 2x2 grid of four equal panels with thin light-grey dividers.
Panel 1 (top-left): front view. Panel 2 (top-right): back view. Panel 3 (bottom-left): left side view. Panel 4 (bottom-right): 45-degree three-quarter view.
The product is the exact item shown in Image 1: {一句话说是什么，例：a navy crewneck sweatshirt with a white daisy print}.
Keep it identical to Image 1 in every panel: {不许改}.
Areas not visible in Image 1 (for example the back) must be plain and conservative — no new prints, logos, pockets or text.
Even soft studio light, no shadows, true-to-life color, product centered and fully visible in each panel, no model, no props, no text labels.
```
- 尺寸 1536x1536 或 2048x2048（方形四格）。出完用 `细节.py --grid 2x2` 拆进 `单张/`。
- 背面、侧面写进身份证「推测」。

## 二、人物定妆板（参考图：Image 1＝主人给的模特原型）
**上半身组**（全身组把 waist-up 换成 full-body head to toe；下半身组换成 waist-down from waist to feet, face not needed）：
```
A character reference sheet of the same person, arranged as a 2x2 grid of four equal panels on a plain light-grey studio background.
Panel 1: front view. Panel 2: 45-degree three-quarter view. Panel 3: 90-degree side profile. Panel 4: back view.
Every panel is a waist-up shot of the person in Image 1, standing naturally with a calm neutral expression.
Preserve exactly from Image 1: {不许改，例：round face, short straight brown bob, small mole under the left eye, warm medium skin tone, average build}.
She wears a plain fitted neutral base layer (solid light grey, no pattern, no logo) instead of her original clothes.
Same even soft light and same camera height in all four panels; consistent proportions across panels; no text labels.
```
- 尺寸 1536x1536；三组各一张。脸部特写要用时单独出一张（正面 head-and-shoulders close-up），同样贴「不许改」。
- 原型只有正面：90° 侧、背面都是推测，背面发型照常理画，写进推测。

**造型三视图**（有固定造型才做；Image 1＝人物原型，Image 2＝造型照片，没有造型照就把造型一条条写进提示词）：
```
A character turnaround sheet of the same person from Image 1, three full-body views side by side on a plain light-grey studio background: front view, side view, back view.
Head to toe in every view, standing naturally, arms relaxed, calm neutral expression.
Preserve exactly from Image 1: {不许改}.
She wears this exact outfit in all three views: {造型，一条条，例：white puffed-sleeve blouse with a heart-shaped cutout; denim pinafore dress with silver chains on the bodice; black lace-up arm cuffs; denim leg warmers with black straps; white chunky sneakers}.
Same even soft light, same camera height and the same scale in all three views; no text labels.
```
- 尺寸 1536x1024；拆格 `--grid 3x1 --prefix 造型 --names 正面,侧面,背面`。造型照只给正面：背面和侧面是推测，写进推测。

**表情板**（人物为主、IP、视频角色才做；Image 1＝人物原型）：
```
An expression sheet of the same person from Image 1, arranged as a 3x2 grid of six equal chest-up portraits on a plain light-grey studio background.
Top row: calm neutral, gentle smile, big open laugh. Bottom row: surprised, sad, angry.
Only the facial expression changes; face shape, features, hairstyle, skin tone and makeup stay exactly the same: {不许改}.
She wears a plain fitted neutral base layer (solid white, no pattern, no logo) in every panel.
Front-facing, same even soft light, same camera height and framing in all six panels; no text labels.
```
- 尺寸 1536x1024；拆格 `--grid 3x2`，单张存 `单张/表情-平静.png` 这样带名字（`--prefix 表情`，`--names 平静,微笑,大笑,惊讶,难过,生气` 直接按名字存）。
- 表情夸张到认不出是同一个人：那一格算不对，重出时在提示词里加 "subtle, natural intensity" 并点名哪一格。

## 三、场景四机位板（参考图：Image 1～2＝主人给的店铺、直播间、房间照片）
```
An environment reference sheet of the same room shown in Image 1, as a 2x2 grid of four camera angles: wide establishing shot, medium shot facing the main wall, a 45-degree angle from the left, and a 45-degree angle from the right.
Keep the room layout, fixed furniture positions, wall colors and window positions exactly as in Image 1: {不许改}.
Same time of day and the same light direction in all panels: {光从哪来，例：daylight from the window on the left}.
No people, no text.
```
- 主人只给一张：另外三个机位都是推测，写进推测。

## 四、上身图（人物＋商品；paint-make 也照这节）
```
Image 1 is the model. Image 2 is the garment.
Dress the person from Image 1 in the garment from Image 2. {景别和姿势，例：waist-up, front view, standing naturally}.
Do not change her face, facial features, skin tone, hairstyle, body shape, pose or identity: {人物不许改}.
The garment must match Image 2 exactly: {商品不许改}. The fabric drapes naturally on the body; fit matches size {码数}.
Background: {景，从场景卡的英文片段取}.
```
参考图顺序：人物板里挑对应景别、角度那一格当 Image 1，商品正面（或对应角度）当 Image 2；最多再加一两张细节当 Image 3、4，总数不超过 6～8 张。

## 五、验收样张（换场景，验一致性）
```
{场景卡的英文片段}. The product in Image 1 appears in this scene: {怎么摆，例：folded on a wooden chair}.
Keep it identical to Image 1: {不许改}.
```
三张换三个差得远的场景（例：白底柔影、居家晨光、城市街头），看换了环境还是不是它。

## 六、改图（一句话改一处）
```
Edit Image 1. Change only {要改的那一处，例：the background, to a warm beige studio wall}.
Keep everything else exactly the same, especially: {不许改}.
```
每一轮都重贴 `{不许改}`；一轮只改一处，改两处分两轮。

## 七、风格母版不生成
风格母版＝主人认可的 2～3 张代表图＋一段话（主色色值、画法、质感、氛围、不要的东西）。出图时把代表图当最后几张参考图，提示词里写「Match the color palette, lighting and texture of Image N; do not copy its subject or layout」。
